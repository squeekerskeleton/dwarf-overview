        Sessions on the telescope
        
    
        Over Wi-Fi, put the DWARF on your home Wi-Fi (STA mode in the DWARFLAB app) or join its hotspot, then click Find my DWARF. Over USB-C, plug it in and switch it on; it shows up as a drive. Files are copied to your library; nothing on the telescope is changed.

            This laptop
            
    
            
    
            
      
            Find and test
            
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
            The test measures each way the DWARF answers right now: round trip, jitter and dropped connections on its control port, its response time, and download speed from its file server. The steadiest one is picked and saved.
            
      
            
      
            Backup connections
            
        
            The DWARF's own hotspot
            
        
            Looking for DWARF hotspots…
            
        
            
        
            Joining the hotspot moves the laptop's Wi-Fi onto the DWARF. If the laptop is also wired to your router, it keeps the internet through the cable; if not, it's offline until you click Back to my Wi-Fi. Claude assessments need the internet; telescope control doesn't.
            
        
            Bluetooth
            
        
            
        
            Bluetooth can show which network the DWARF is on and its address, and ask it to rejoin its saved Wi-Fi. It can't carry control or pictures. Needs Chrome or Edge, and the DWARFLAB phone app closed.
